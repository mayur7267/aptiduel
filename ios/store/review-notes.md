2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Apticks is a game for college students in India who are preparing for campus placement tests. Aptitude practice is usually a dull, solo grind. Apticks turns it into live 1v1 duels: 5 questions (quantitative, logical, verbal) in 3 minutes, and speed counts. A rating shows how test-ready a player is, and players can see how they rank among students at their college. The audience is college students, mostly 18 to 23.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP (NO LOGIN NEEDED)
The app opens as a guest automatically, so no credentials or sample files are needed.
- After the short intro, tap "Find my first match". You get a 5-question duel against another player and then a result with your rating change.
- Home also has practice sets for company-style tests. The Daily tab has one Daily Challenge a day. The Ranks tab shows the leaderboard, and tapping a player opens their public profile. The Profile tab shows rating and stats.
- Optional account: Profile > Account > enter an email > we send a code > enter it. An account is only needed for live friend duels ("Duel a friend" on Home), where you send a room link and a friend joins. The code goes to the player's own inbox, so the screen recording shows this flow from start to finish.
- Sign out and Delete account are both in Profile > Account. Delete account removes the name, college, email and rating.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase: database, guest and email-code sign-in, and realtime for live duels.
- Vercel: hosts the website and challenge links (aptiduel.vercel.app).
- Gmail (SMTP): sends the sign-in code emails. In-app feedback reaches us by email through Gmail or Resend.
- Apple StoreKit: only to ask happy players for an App Store rating.
There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no AI services and no payment processor.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. The practice content is written for Indian placement tests (rupees, Indian company names, Indian colleges), but nothing is region-locked.

6. REGULATED OR THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
This is not a regulated industry. The practice questions are original, generated by our own tooling, and no company's real test content is used. Company names only label practice sets. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any company.

OTHER
- The app is free. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid content.
- The only text a player writes is a display name, and they pick a college from a list. There is no chat, photo or free-text sharing. Names appear on the leaderboard and public profiles. Players can report a problem from Profile > Help & contact or by shaking the phone.
